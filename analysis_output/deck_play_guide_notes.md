# Oswald Notes

## General Philosophy
Generally trying to dump hand to not lose early and establishing a workshop, get greedy and develop more workshop/quill/magnifying glass when there’s time, and win eventually. Also, once in a while you can win without Tamatoa and/or Dime even in slower matchups so sometimes you just have to ink those.

Cards that have been in the list at some points:
- Hide Away
- Might Solve a Mystery
- Maui 5
- Tamatoa 6
- Brom Bones

## General Concepts

### OTP
Always keep Oswald. In Steel matchups, try to find what will be your weakest turn and just play him that turn and ink and hope for the best. Can get a little greedier with slower hands since you’re more likely to have a turn to just play Workshop and do nothing else.

### OTD
Probs should never keep Oswald unless you know it’s a control deck, but he’s the goat so sometimes I get greedy. Sometimes need to accept that you just may have no Workshop/card draw and hopefully your small dudes get you there.

### Both
Generally blind you need to alter for aggro matchups, sometimes you’ll be put in a bad spot against control decks but you’re so favored you’ll be fine in games 2 and 3. Coil is probably the most important card to keep because it enables your removal for the whole game, and even against something like RB Shark, you need 3 Coil triggers to get him so it’s more important in that matchup then you’d expect. Keep an eye out for the turn 3/4 rush Scrooge lines, especially these builds with extra 1-drop items. Setting up a Workshop on turn 3 and playing Scrooge is great, usually can be done on turn 4 even when you had to play Belle or Liquidator on earlier turns.

## GY
Ideal line: Liquidator -> Coil or Belle + Tool + Workshop -> Sisu -> Sisu -> Sisu -> shift Sisu

On the play there’s more flexibility with the best line, but you really need to set up Coil and big Sisu so keep big Sisu in the opener if you got it and don’t need to alter too hard for Belle/Liquidator.

Oswald has a lot of value behind Liquidator or on the play because it can apply lore pressure at some point and also can give free value since most of the items do something extra helpful in that matchup. Even flipping a Ruby Chromicon can make Oswald into a potential challenger.

Cards worth considering for the matchup:
- Hide Away
- Brom Bones
- Nani’s Payback
- Rush characters

Cards to drop:
- Workshop
- Magnifying Glass
- Brawl

## GS
Ideal line:
- whatever -> Coil -> Sisu -> Sisu -> Workshop + Pawp
- or
- Belle + Tool + Magnifying Glass -> Magnifying Glass activation -> Brawl -> keep using Magnifying Glass until you get big Sisu

This matchup is a little weird because you need to alter for Diablo shift, and eventually you’ll get max Goof Muses locked. Magnifying Glass is a great mitigator since it’s not drawing, but you need to decide quick if you can try and keep enough cards in hand so that they can’t discard your whole hand or if you’re not going to have a hand, just commit to having no cards and blank their Prince John/discard cards. The second plan is especially good against versions still on Hypnotize and Chip(or Dale? whichever one discards). So sometimes dumping the hand isn’t bad, but can be a hard commit. Ink Runner giving Alert is a huge help, that means you only really need to have removal for first Diablo and then hopefully you can stick a guy and challenge. Vitalisphere is huge and probably worth playing on turn 1 a lot of the time also with the lore pressure that can come from the new Diablo and Prince John.

Cards worth considering for the matchup:
- Full Magnifying Glass
- Jeb Cookie
- Merryweather
- Full Brawl
- Tam 6

Cards to drop:
- Oswald
- Big Sisu
- Workshop

Mostly just want to be lower curve, dump hand, and be able to have bigger bodies. Big Sisu sweeper is great, but you always have to plan for the first one getting stuck in your hand and discarded. Can also cut some little Sisu if you go full Brawl and add Jeb.

## RB / BP / RP
Grouping these together because you just want to get Oswald value and cheat out Workshop. Need to still value Belle/Liquidator to deny early value for BP and RP, but aside from that you should mostly be okay. Try to have some strength boosting stuff to give more ways to remove x/5 guys like Scrooge and Shark. Also against Shark, prioritize Coil effects so you still have a way to beat the early double Shark lines. Matchups are good so I’ve been cutting Hide Aways, but those could also be helpful to dodge early Manor/Library setups which can be a little rough if Scrooge/Vitalispheres aren’t showing up.

Cards worth considering for the matchup:
- 4th Oswald
- Maui
- Hide Away
- Tam 6
- Let It Go (only for Shark and Leviathan really)
- GSD

Cards to drop:
- some Brawl/Sisus
- Ruby Chrom
- Liquidator (he’s good into RP Flynn though)

## AS Songs
So many different builds out there but they all require Coil and probably Quill to clear. Beyond the Horizon can make things rough.

## AS / PY Aggro
Both of these matchups just really want you to play guys every turn, you don’t need to set up Coil really so that can help, but without adding a bunch of tech cards, I’m just accepting this matchup is winnable but bad.
